Technicomp Labs is the independent workshop of [Paul D. Martin, Ph.D.](https://pauldmartin.phd) It is the home of Technicomp Benchtop Linux, and of performance research on current hardware and a collection of high-end systems from every decade.

**Benchtop Linux.** The lab's main project is [Technicomp Benchtop Linux](/benchtop/), the operating system for the technical workbench. It is an immutable GNOME desktop with an LTS core, now in alpha.

**Research.** The lab's performance research is published openly. The [LLM Performance Engineering Notebook](https://github.com/pauldmartinphd/llm-performance-engineering-notebook) documents experiments on the inference speed limits of large Mixture-of-Experts models, including the hypotheses that testing did not support.

**The collection.** [The collection](/collection/) consists of high-end computers and game consoles from the late 1970s through the 2000s, restored and upgraded to the limits of their era.

**The blog.** [Technical posts](/blog/) cover operating systems, performance and benchmarking, applied machine learning, security, hardware and firmware, and restoration and upgrade work. Posts rely on measurements from [the lab](/lab/) where measurements apply.

Technicomp Labs is personal, independent work, separate from Dr. Martin's [professional research and expert witness practice](https://pauldmartin.phd).
