## Electronics and repair bench

The main bench is set up for board-level work. It has a programmable bench power supply, soldering and hot-air rework stations, an oscilloscope, and parts organizers stocked with the components that restorations and upgrades consume. An open-frame test bench beside it holds the motherboard under diagnosis, so boards can be brought up and probed outside a case.

## Compute

Current hardware lives in a mobile rack with a virtualization host, hot-swap storage, and battery backup, alongside the custom machines used for the lab's performance research. The largest is Galactus, the EPYC inference server. Measurements from these machines are published in the [LLM Performance Engineering Notebook](https://github.com/pauldmartinphd/llm-performance-engineering-notebook).

## Staging and burn-in

A second bench stages machines between arrival and restoration. Network switches and a KVM let several systems be brought up at once, and finished machines are shelved below.
