Updates that don't break your bench.

Benchtop Linux is a GNOME desktop for workstations. Its core follows long-term-support releases and the desktop runs GNOME Oldstable, so the system stays stable while security fixes still arrive quickly. Every update is a snapshot you can roll back. The desktop stays responsive under heavy load, and it's ready out of the box for every thread of your work: AI, virtualization, software development, system administration, security, reverse engineering, and content creation.

“Let other people be your beta testers.”
